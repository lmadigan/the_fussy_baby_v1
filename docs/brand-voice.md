# The Fussy Baby Brand Voice

Write to one tired parent who wants a clear next step.

- Warm, calm, and direct.
- Use plain English. Define clinical terms and acronyms the first time they appear.
- Prefer short, action-first sentences that are easy to scan.
- Reassure without minimizing what the parent is seeing.
- Be honest about uncertainty without sounding legal or defensive.
- Treat lived experience as valuable context while distinguishing it from established evidence.
- Explain why a step matters, then say what to do.
- Never diagnose, promise an outcome, or imply that one symptom proves a cause.

Good: "Look for signs of oversupply: coughing at letdown, frequent spit-up, or green frothy stools. One sign alone does not confirm it."

Avoid: "Evaluate the infant for symptoms consistent with hyperlactation syndrome."
